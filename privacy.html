菜的那些事儿 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月21日

菜的那些事儿是一款讲蔬菜来历故事的科普 App。我们对隐私的原则很简单：我们不收集、不保存您的任何个人信息。

  • 无账号：本 App 不需要注册或登录，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱等身份信息。
  • 内容全在本地：24 篇蔬菜故事文章随 App 一起打包，离线即可阅读，不需要联网下载。
  • 进度只存本机：您的集卡和答题进度只保存在您自己的设备上，不上传任何服务器。
  • 拍照识别会联网发送照片：只有当您主动使用「拍照查找」时，您拍摄或选择的照片才会被发送至植物识别服务 Pl@ntNet 进行识别；我们自己不保存这些照片，也不将其与您的身份关联，不用于追踪或广告。除这一功能外，App 的其余内容全部离线运行。
  • 无广告、无追踪：本 App 不含广告，不接入任何广告或统计 SDK，不进行用户追踪。
  • 删除即清除：删除本 App，其全部本地数据会随之从设备上删除。

Cai De Na Xie Shi Er Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This app tells the origin stories of everyday vegetables. Our privacy principle is simple: we do not collect or store any of your personal information.

  • No accounts: The app requires no sign-up or login and collects no name, phone number, email, or other identity information.
  • Content is built in: All 24 vegetable story articles are bundled with the app and can be read fully offline.
  • Progress stays on-device: Your collection cards and quiz progress are stored only on your own device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Photo identification sends your photo online: Only when you actively use Photo Lookup is the photo you take or pick sent to the Pl@ntNet plant-identification service for identification. We do not store these photos ourselves, do not link them to your identity, and never use them for tracking or advertising. Everything else in the app works fully offline.
  • No ads, no tracking: The app contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data: Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your device.
