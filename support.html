这是什么 App？

它讲 24 种常见蔬菜的来历故事：每种菜从哪里来、什么时候来到中国、名字是怎么来的。按本土、胡来（古代传入）、番来（美洲传入）、洋来（近代传入）四条线整理，每篇带五色、五味两个文化标签，读完还有 3 道小题和一张可以收藏的集卡。

需要注册或联网吗？

不需要注册。无账号、无广告，全部内容随 App 打包，离线就能读；只有「拍照查找」的识别需要联网。

「拍照查找」能用吗？

能用。在 App 里拍一张或从相册选一张蔬菜照片，就会联网识别并给出最可能的几种候选，点候选就能读它的故事。识别结果仅供参考；不要凭识别结果采食野生植物。也可以用页面下方的「按名字查找」，或在首页顶部按菜名、别名搜索。

文章里的营养一格为什么写着「数据核对中」？

营养数值我们要逐条对着《中国食物成分表》核对，核完一条上线一条。在核对完成之前宁可空着，也不写一个没核准的数字。

集卡和答题进度会丢吗？

进度保存在本机，正常使用不会丢。但它不会同步到云端：删除 App 或更换手机后，进度无法恢复，需要重新收集。

五色、五味是什么意思？

五色（青、赤、黄、白、黑）和五味（酸、苦、甘、辛、咸）是中国传统的饮食文化分类。在本 App 里，五色对应蔬菜天然的色素，五味按真实味道标注，只当文化和味道的标签来讲故事，不涉及任何功效说法。

Support

What does this app do?

It tells the origin stories of 24 everyday vegetables — where each one came from, when it arrived in China, and how it got its name. Stories are organized along four routes (native, ancient Silk Road introductions, introductions from the Americas, and modern introductions). Each article carries five-color and five-flavor cultural tags, 3 quiz questions, and a collectible card.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. There are no ads. All content is bundled with the app and works offline; only photo identification needs an internet connection.

Does photo lookup work?

Yes. Take or pick a photo of a vegetable in the app and it will identify it online and offer the most likely matches; tap one to read its story. Results are for reference only — never eat a wild plant based on an identification result. You can also use the name search on that page, or search by name or alias at the top of the home page.

Why does the nutrition box say "being verified"?

Every nutrition figure is checked one by one against the China Food Composition Tables before it is shown. Until a figure is verified, we would rather leave the box empty than show a number we have not confirmed.

Will I lose my cards and quiz progress?

Progress is stored on your device and is kept during normal use, but it is not synced to the cloud. Deleting the app or switching phones means starting the collection over.

What are the five colors and five flavors?

They are traditional Chinese dietary-culture categories. In this app, the five colors correspond to vegetables' natural pigments and the five flavors are labeled by actual taste. They are used purely as cultural and taste labels for storytelling, with no health or efficacy claims.
